# First Claim
Single connected river valley. River camp at (0,0,8), river bank east, visible mountain north. Fictional claim currency (C). A 45–90 minute first campaign is a design target, not a measured duration.

## Progression and complete scope
Player begins with pan, shovel and vial; finite deterministic sediment batches provide sufficient gold for classifier and detector. Manual pan stages: scoop → immerse → alternate swirl → tilt wash → collect. Tailings retain unrecovered fine gold. Classifier improves recovery and wash throughput.
Detector measures true distance/depth to authored depleted-once targets. Follow samples along the uphill path; acquire mining kit. Mine is a bounded excavatable voxel field with collision rebuilt from remaining cells, supports validated against adjacent rock and ground. Widening and barrow clearance are real collision checks. Local unsupported span can progress through warning to small clearable rubble falls; no vital reward removed.
Workshop: sieve ore batches, refine copper and tin, alloy bronze, cast gear, polish quartz, refine gold and assemble pendant. Data-driven checked atomic transactions; contracts unlocked by resource/equipment eligibility, single payout. Guaranteed final crystal and critical ore. Milestone contracts unlock final seam, signature pendant and specimen display end campaign, continue enabled.

## Input and presentation
WASD move, mouse look, E interact, 1–5 tools, left mouse use, right mouse alternate, Q/R pan swirl, F tilt, B support, L lamp, Tab journal, Escape pause. Remappable detail and UI subject to usability review. Reduced motion and simple pan input share economy. Independent sound categories. Separate save slots campaign/capture/test.

## Verification
Meaningful accounting and persistence tests plus actual engine visuals and normal-play full run. Scripted tests clearly labeled, never substituted for player journey. Asset batches must be checked in engine before advancing. Performance measured on M4 16 GB at actual chosen renderer/resolution. No benchmark parity claim without visual comparison.
